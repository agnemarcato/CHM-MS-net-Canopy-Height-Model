# Summary 
Canopy Height Models (CHMs) are useful tools for a wide variety of ecological, economic, geospatial applications. Moreover, CHMs provide crucial information regarding the vertical and horizontal structure of vegetation. High-resolution CHMs are usually produced from airborne or terrestrial lidar (Light Detection and Ranging). (Allred et al. 2025). LiDAR data is expensive and less available than satellite imagery. Therefore, this program leverages a convolutional neural networks (MS-NET) to predict CHMs from satellite imagery.
